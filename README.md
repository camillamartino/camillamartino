
# Camilla Martino

**MSc Student in Space Engineering | Politecnico di Milano**

I am a Master's student in Space Engineering at Politecnico di Milano, with a Bachelor's degree in Aerospace Engineering and a strong interest in spacecraft systems engineering, assembly, integration, verification and testing (AIVT), and space propulsion.

Through academic projects and my involvement in PoliSpace, I have gained hands-on experience in requirements engineering, subsystem integration, engineering design, and multidisciplinary teamwork.

My interests lie in bridging theoretical engineering analysis with practical spacecraft development, from early design phases to system-level integration and verification.

---

## Experience

### PoliSpace | CubeSat Project
**Systems Engineering & AIVT – Software and Electronics**

As a member of the PoliSpace CubeSat team, I contribute to the development and integration of spacecraft subsystems, with a particular focus on software and electronics.

My responsibilities include:

- Definition and management of flight software and On-Board Computer (OBC) requirements.
- Integration of the software subsystem with the Electrical Power System (EPS).
- Definition of subsystem interfaces and development of integration procedures.
- Integration and verification of spacecraft subsystems using a flatsat configuration.

---

## Selected Projects

### MAVEN | Space Mission Reverse Engineering
**Academic Group Project | Politecnico di Milano**

A multidisciplinary reverse engineering study of NASA's Mars Atmosphere and Volatile Evolution (MAVEN) mission.

Contributed collaboratively to the analysis of mission architecture, spacecraft configuration, and subsystem design, gaining experience in understanding the interactions between different spacecraft systems.

**Areas:** Spacecraft Design, Mission Analysis, Systems Engineering

### STARK | 100 kN Solid Rocket Motor
**Academic Group Project | Politecnico di Milano**

Design and analysis of a 100 kN solid rocket motor featuring a BATES grain configuration.

My primary contributions focused on the thermal management system, specifically:

- Cooling jacket design and engineering analysis.
- Design of the tanks associated with the cooling circuit.
- Coordination with other subsystem design activities to ensure compatibility with the overall propulsion system.

**Areas:** Space Propulsion, Thermal Management, Engineering Design
